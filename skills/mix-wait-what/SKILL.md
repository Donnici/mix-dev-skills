Wait, I don't understand where you've got to. Re-pitch your last message:

- Start with one or two sentences of context: what we were doing and why this point matters.
- Write in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: short sentences, one idea each, common words,
  active voice.
- Use the project's glossary terms (`mix-dev-skills/glossary.md`, resolved by the `mix-scope` skill).
  Define any other term the first time you use it, or replace it with plain words.
- End with the one thing you need from me, if anything.
