# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in mix-dev-skills (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) gets a
respectful, harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent; most disagreements are about trade-offs, not people.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, with evidence: a failing case, a transcript, a diff.
- Credit other people's work. This project is built on Spec Kit, Matt Pocock's skills and other
  MIT projects, and new contributions keep the same standard of attribution.
- Write in English or Portuguese; both are welcome.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sexualised language and imagery.
- Public or private harassment, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Submitting skills or prompts designed to harm users of the project (prompt injection, data
  exfiltration, hidden network calls). Report those through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this
code, and may block contributors who do so repeatedly or seriously.

Report problems privately to **bruno.dias.donnici@gmail.com**. Every report is read and kept
confidential, and you will get an answer within 7 days.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space (the repository, its issues and pull requests) and
when someone represents the project in public.
